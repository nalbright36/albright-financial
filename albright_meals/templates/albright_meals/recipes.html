The list defaults to All recipes (every space you see, plus recipes shared with you personally). "Showing: All recipes" narrows to Mine (your Personal space), a circle, a person who shared with you, Shared with me, or the Recipe library (?from=, remembered). A search box and one row of filter chips (All, Staples, Favorites, Not rated, the most-used tags, Under 30 min - views._recipe_chips) that keep the search and the source; "More filters" has the full rating filters (who rated it, which levels, "We both love"). Everything is in the URL, so a filter can be bookmarked. A card that isn't yours shows a small badge (circle name or person). The header space switcher is on the other Meals pages, not here - this dropdown is the Recipes filter and does not change the remembered space. Each card: the title (the card's link - it covers the card), a ⋯ menu (Share, Add ingredients to groceries, Edit, Delete, and where it came from), a one-line description, time and servings, then my rating chip (tap: a small menu of the levels, saved without opening the recipe - static/meals/ratings.js), the space's other stamps, and two tags. The menu, the chip and the stamps sit above the card's link, so using them never opens the recipe. {% endcomment %}
