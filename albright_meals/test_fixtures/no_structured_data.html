My Tomato Soup

By Sam Cook. This is the soup I make every winter. Serves 4. Takes about 30 minutes.

You'll need

2 tbsp butter
1 onion, chopped
28 oz can whole tomatoes
2 cups vegetable stock

How to make it

Melt the butter and soften the onion.

Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.

Blend until smooth.

Hidden: 1 cup heavy cream
Also hidden: stir in parmesan
