{% comment %} The Attachments section of a Planner task (view panel, edit form) or a note (albright_labs.attachments.section -> att). Without JavaScript the form posts and comes back. Under it: the uploader's storage ("312 MB of 1 GB used" - amber at 80%, red at 100%); at 100% Add files is turned off with a note (att.storage, albright_labs.storage).
