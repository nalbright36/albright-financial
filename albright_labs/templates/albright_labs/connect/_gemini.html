The Gemini app can only use outside services through custom apps, which Google offers in Gemini Apps Developer Mode - and not on every account. Gems can't call outside services.

If your account has Developer Mode:

  1. Open Gemini on the web and go to Settings › Gemini Apps Developer Mode (or gemini.google.com/devmode), and turn it on.
  2. Connect an MCP server, and paste this as the MCP Server URL: {% include "albright_labs/connect/_copy.html" with value=links.mcp id="gemini-mcp" %}
  3. For sign-in, choose Dynamic Client Registration.
  4. Gemini opens a page on this site: choose Full access or Read only and click Approve.
  5. In a chat, paste the instructions below first.

No Developer Mode? The Gemini app can't connect to this site for you. Use the Other / any AI tab's method with any assistant or script that can make web requests.
