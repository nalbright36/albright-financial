Grok connects through a custom connector on grok.com.

  1. On a computer, open Grok on the web and go to grok.com/connectors.
  2. Click New Connector, then Custom.
  3. Paste this as the MCP server URL: {% include "albright_labs/connect/_copy.html" with value=links.mcp id="grok-mcp" %}
  4. Finish the sign-in: Grok opens a page on this site - choose Full access or Read only and click Approve.
  5. Start a chat, paste the instructions below, and ask Grok to use Albright Labs.

On Grok Business or Enterprise, a team admin has to add the connector first. If the connector won't sign in, use the Other / any AI tab instead.
