Entry Signal

A "buy" signal opens a long call. A "sell"/bearish signal opens a long put. Once open, a position can only be closed by Risk Management below — never by an opposite signal. Positions are also always force-closed within 2 days of expiration, regardless of your other settings.

Options Structure
Position Sizing

Contracts: enter a contract count (e.g. 2). Dollar amount: enter a total dollar budget — divided by premium x100 (each contract = 100 shares) to get contract count. % of buying power / cash: same, based on a percentage of your account instead of a fixed dollar figure.

Risk Management

All percentages here measure the option's own premium moving, not the underlying stock's price — premium moves far more than the stock itself, so these numbers are usually much larger than you'd set for an equivalent stock strategy. Positions are always force-closed within 2 days of expiration regardless of these settings.
