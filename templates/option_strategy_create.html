Position Sizing

Contracts: enter a contract count (e.g. 2). Dollar amount: enter a total dollar budget — divided by premium x100 (each contract = 100 shares) to get contract count. % of buying power / cash: same, based on a percentage of your account instead of a fixed dollar figure.

Risk Management

All percentages here measure the option's own premium moving, not the underlying stock's price — premium moves far more than the stock itself, so these numbers are usually much larger than you'd set for an equivalent stock strategy.
