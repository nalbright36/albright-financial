{# Google's steps - checked against aistudio.google.com/docs (API keys, billing, spend caps, models), Nov 2026. #}
  1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Set up billing on the Billing page ("Set up billing" - a $5 minimum prepay). This is separate from a Google AI Pro or Gemini app subscription - you pay per use, usually cents per chat. (New accounts start on a limited free tier, which also works here until you hit its limits.)
  3. Recommended: set a monthly spend cap on the Spend page (Monthly spend cap › Edit spend cap).
  4. Create a key on the API keys page and copy it. It starts with AIza.
  5. Paste it below and press Connect.
