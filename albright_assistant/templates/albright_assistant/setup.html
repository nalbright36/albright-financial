Your AI assistant

Chat with an AI from any page - the round button at the bottom right. It can look things up and make changes in your apps for you (it asks first before deleting or sharing anything). It uses your own AI provider account and developer API key: you pay the provider directly, usually cents per chat, and this site never pays for or sees your bill.

This is separate from API keys, which connect an outside AI app (like the ChatGPT or Claude apps) to your account.
