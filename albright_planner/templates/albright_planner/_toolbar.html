Its button carries a dot while something is filtered out (views.is_filtered). New item quick add first; "More options" brings the full form in below it (new/?panel=1). Without JavaScript the view switch and the arrows are plain links, the jump form shows the date field and a Go button, and the dialog buttons are links to the Planner settings page and the New item page. Keys (desktop): D / W / M switch views, T today, N New item, Left / Right previous or next. {% endcomment %}
